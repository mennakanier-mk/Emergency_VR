Emergency VR is an immersive virtual reality simulation built in Unity 3D. The experience places the user inside a fully reconstructed 3D model of Talaat Harb Street, one of Cairo's most iconic and busiest urban corridors located in the heart of downtown.

The scene authentically replicates the chaotic atmosphere of Egyptian street life — congested traffic, honking vehicles, pedestrians, motorcycles, bicycles, horse-drawn carriages and the ambient noise of a busy urban day. Landmarks such as a Police Station (مركز شرطة) and a Mosque are clearly visible along the street, anchoring the scene in a recognizable Egyptian context.

The narrative unfolds in real time: a child is struck by a car in the middle of heavy traffic. Bystanders react. An ambulance is dispatched. A paramedic arrives, performs first-aid procedures including applying an oxygen mask, and carries the child to the ambulance for transport to a hospital. The entire sequence is designed to raise awareness about road safety and demonstrate correct emergency medical response protocols.

Section 02
Scene Narrative & Story Flow
01

🌆 Scene Establishes — Talaat Harb Street

The user is immersed in a bustling 3D recreation of Talaat Harb Street in downtown Cairo. Cars, motorcycles, bicycles, tuk-tuks, and a horse-drawn carriage fill the road. Ambient sounds of car horns, engines, and street noise create a highly realistic urban environment. Pedestrians walk along the sidewalks.

02

🚗 Heavy Traffic Chaos (الزحمة والضوضاء)

Multiple vehicles move along the road simultaneously — cars driven by the road driver character, motorcycles, bicycles, and the traditional horse carriage. The traffic network system controls vehicle paths and speeds. The noise level and visual congestion simulates the real experience of being in Cairo city center.

03

💥 The Accident — Child Gets Hit

A young boy (the "Accident Boy" character) is struck by a moving vehicle. The accident car script triggers the collision event. A blood pool effect appears at the scene. The child falls and remains motionless on the ground, creating an urgent situation in the middle of the busy street.

04

😱 Bystanders React (المارة)

Nearby pedestrians — including a veiled woman and an elderly man in traditional Egyptian galabiya — stop walking and gather around the accident scene. The AccidentBystander script controls their reactions. Their panic and attention-drawing behavior mirrors realistic crowd response to emergencies in public spaces.

05

🚨 Ambulance Dispatched (الإسعاف يصل)

An ambulance responds to the accident. The AmbulanceResponse script controls its arrival — the vehicle navigates through congested traffic with its siren active (emergency audio: em.flac). The ambulance parks near the accident site, its doors open and the paramedic steps out.

06

🩺 First Aid Administered (الإسعافات الأولية)

The Emergency Man / Paramedic character approaches the injured child. The ParamedicRig script positions him correctly. An OxygenMask is placed over the child's face (OxygenMask.cs / OxygenMaskModel.cs). The paramedic performs initial first-aid assessment and stabilization procedures.

07

🤝 Child Carried to Ambulance

The BoyCarry script triggers the carrying animation — the paramedic lifts the injured child and walks toward the ambulance. The RunExtender script supports smooth locomotion. The child is placed inside the ambulance, ready for hospital transport.

08

🏥 Ambulance Departs — Scene Ends

The ambulance drives away from the scene with the child secured inside. The VR experience concludes, having demonstrated proper emergency response from accident occurrence through first-aid treatment and patient evacuation — reinforcing road safety and emergency awareness.

Section 07
Core Scripts & Logic
AmbulanceResponse.cs
Controls ambulance navigation, arrival timing, door open/close and departure sequence after patient pickup.
AccidentCar.cs
Triggers the collision event with the child and manages post-accident vehicle behavior and stops.
ParamedicRig.cs
Positions and animates the paramedic character through first-aid procedures step by step.
OxygenMask.cs / OxygenMaskModel.cs
Handles the oxygen mask attachment to the injured child's face during the first-aid sequence.
BoyCarry.cs
Manages the animation of the paramedic carrying the child from the accident site to the ambulance.
BloodPool.cs
Spawns and manages the blood pool visual effect at the accident site to indicate injury severity.
TrafficNetwork.cs
Defines the road network graph. All vehicles follow nodes/paths defined by this system.
TrafficRoad.cs / TrafficPath.cs
Define individual road segments and vehicle path waypoints throughout Talaat Harb Street.
AccidentBystander.cs
Controls crowd reaction behavior — pedestrians stop, face the accident, and simulate shock responses.
PedestrianWalker.cs
Animates pedestrian characters walking along sidewalks before the accident occurs.
RoadDriver.cs
Controls driving character behavior including steering, speed management, and traffic path following.
CarExitPassenger.cs
Handles passenger exit animation from stopped vehicles after the accident event is triggered.
VRLink.cs / VRLinkController.cs
Links VR headset/phone input to the Unity scene for immersive head-tracking and user movement.
SimpleStereoVR.cs
Enables stereoscopic rendering for Google Cardboard and mobile VR device compatibility.
BicycleWheels.cs
Rotates bicycle wheels procedurally based on movement speed for realistic wheel animation.
AnimatedVehicleMover.cs
Moves vehicles along predefined animated paths through the traffic network smoothly.

Section 09
Educational Objectives

Emergency VR is designed as an educational awareness tool targeting road safety and emergency response preparedness. By placing users in the middle of a realistic accident scenario, the simulation achieves several key learning outcomes:

🚸 Road Safety Awareness: The chaotic traffic environment of Talaat Harb Street demonstrates the dangers of congested urban roads, particularly for pedestrians and children. Users viscerally experience how quickly accidents can happen in everyday street conditions.

🩺 Emergency First Aid Education: The step-by-step paramedic sequence — oxygen mask application, patient stabilization, and careful transport — models correct first-aid procedures for accident victims, educating viewers on proper emergency protocols.

🚨 Emergency Services Awareness: The simulation highlights the role of ambulance services, paramedics, and emergency personnel in saving lives. It demonstrates that calling emergency services promptly and clearing the path for responders is critical.

🌍 Cultural & Urban Context: Set specifically in Cairo's Talaat Harb Street with authentic Egyptian characters, vehicles, and landmarks (mosque, police station), the simulation is designed to resonate with Egyptian audiences and address local urban safety challenges.
